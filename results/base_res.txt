# TellAScam Baseline Results

## Dataset

UCI SMS Spam Collection

After preprocessing:
- 5,169 unique messages
- Exact duplicates removed before splitting
- No missing values
- No conflicting labels

## Split

Stratified 80/20 train-test split.

- Training samples: 4,135
- Testing samples: 1,034

## Model

TF-IDF Vectorizer + Logistic Regression

## Results

| Metric | Ham | Spam |
|---|---:|---:|
| Precision | 0.96 | 0.96 |
| Recall | 1.00 | 0.75 |
| F1 | 0.98 | 0.84 |

Overall accuracy: 0.9642

## Confusion Matrix

| | Predicted Ham | Predicted Spam |
|---|---:|---:|
| Actual Ham | 899 | 4 |
| Actual Spam | 33 | 98 |

## Initial Observation

The baseline achieves high overall accuracy and high spam precision,
but spam recall is considerably lower. The classifier correctly
identifies 98 of 131 spam messages while missing 33.

Future experiments should investigate whether spam recall can be
improved without causing an excessive increase in false positives.
